## 1. Go-to-Market Strategy

### GTM Philosophy

ViralScopes is a **product-led growth** business with a content marketing engine. The product is the primary acquisition channel — great UX, a free plan that delivers real value, and shareable outputs (exports, viral scores) drive word-of-mouth. Paid acquisition is secondary and only deployed after organic channels are validated.

### Phase 1 — Beta (Months 1–4)

**Goal:** Validate product-market fit with 50 paying beta customers.

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| Closed beta | 20 hand-picked creators and agency strategists (warm outreach) |
| Feedback cadence | Weekly 30-min calls with all beta users |
| Beta pricing | 40% discount on Starter and Professional (converts to full price at launch) |
| NPS gate | Do not proceed to public launch until NPS > 35 from beta cohort |
| Content: launch announcement | "What I built and why" — founder story on Twitter/X and LinkedIn |
| Content: behind-the-scenes | Build-in-public posts documenting the journey (weekly) |

### Phase 2 — Public Launch (Month 5)

**Goal:** 200 signups in the first 2 weeks.

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| Product Hunt launch | Prepare 60 days in advance. Hunter relationship. Teaser posts. Day-of coordination in creator communities. |
| Twitter/X thread | "I analysed 50,000 YouTube videos. Here's what actually makes content go viral." (Data-led, shareable) |
| Hacker News Show HN | Technical audience; focus on the AI architecture and ethical constraint |
| YouTube/creator community outreach | Post in r/NewTubers, r/YouTubers, Creator Economy Discord communities |
| Email to beta waitlist | Convert interest list built during beta |
| Partnership announcement | If any creator educator partnership is secured by launch |

### Phase 3 — Growth (Months 6–18)

**Goal:** Scale to 1,000 paying customers. Lower CAC below £150.

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| SEO content engine | 3 articles per week targeting high-intent creator keywords |
| YouTube channel | Weekly insights videos ("Why this video scored 87" format) |
| Email newsletter | Weekly "Viral Insights" newsletter using real ViralScopes data |
| Affiliate programme | Launch at v1.5 with 20% recurring commission for 12 months |
| Agency partnerships | Direct outreach to 50 top creator agencies in UK and US |
| Paid social (test) | Small budget (£500/month) testing on Twitter/X and YouTube pre-roll |

### Phase 4 — Expansion (Month 18+)

**Goal:** Enterprise motion and international expansion.

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| Enterprise sales | Dedicated account executive for organisations > £500/month |
| Vertical content | Spanish-language creator content ahead of v2.0 multi-language launch |
| Industry reports | Annual "State of YouTube Creator Economy" research report |
| Conference presence | VidCon, CreatorIQ Summit, Social Media Marketing World |
| Partner ecosystem | Integrations with Notion, Airtable, Slack to expand distribution |

## 2. Acquisition Channels

### SEO

**Why:** High-intent creator search traffic converts well and has near-zero marginal CAC at scale.

**Keyword categories:**

| Category | Example keywords | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| Tool comparison | "VidIQ vs TubeBuddy", "best YouTube analytics tool 2026" | High purchase intent |
| How-to creator | "how to find viral YouTube video ideas", "how to write YouTube title" | Content-led, mid-funnel |
| Data/research | "YouTube algorithm 2026", "what makes a YouTube video go viral" | Top-of-funnel, data-seekers |
| Creator business | "YouTube channel growth strategy", "how to grow YouTube channel fast" | Creator-native, broad |

**SEO approach:**
- Target long-tail keywords (< 5,000 searches/month) with high creator purchase intent
- Each article targets one primary keyword and 3–5 secondary keywords
- Articles include real ViralScopes data where possible (unique proprietary angle)
- Internal linking between all articles and product features
- Build backlinks via HARO, creator podcast appearances, and industry reports

**Target:** 10,000 organic sessions/month by Month 12.

### Content Marketing

**Blog format: "The Analysis"** — each post is a deep analysis of a YouTube trend or viral video, using ViralScopes data. Format: specific insight + supporting data + original takeaways.

> Example: "Why every 'I Quit My Job' video goes viral (and what the data actually says)" — using ViralScopes viral score breakdown and hook classification data from 200 videos on the topic.

**Frequency:** 3 articles per week. Target: 150 articles by Month 12.

**Distribution:** Blog → Twitter/X thread summary → LinkedIn post → Newsletter → Community sharing.

### Social Media

| Platform | Strategy | Content type | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twitter/X | Primary distribution platform. Follow creator economy conversation. | Data insights, product updates, build-in-public | Daily |
| LinkedIn | Agency and brand team audience. More polished. | Case studies, industry insights, product news | 3×/week |
| YouTube | Own channel showing creators how to use the platform and interpret data | Tutorial, insight, founder | Weekly |
| TikTok (Phase 3) | Short data insights. Test format. | 60-second viral score breakdowns | 3×/week |

### Email Marketing

**List segments:**

| Segment | Content | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Free users | Activation tips, upgrade prompts, weekly trend digest | Weekly |
| Paying users (Starter/Pro) | Deep feature tips, trend data, new feature announcements | Weekly |
| Agency users | Agency-specific insights, client reporting tips | Weekly |
| Enterprise leads | Case studies, ROI data, product roadmap | Bi-weekly |
| Churned users | Win-back sequence (1, 7, 30 days post-churn) | Triggered |

**Weekly newsletter: "Viral Insights"**
Format: 5-minute read. Three sections:
1. The week's highest-scoring video in one niche + analysis of why
2. One emerging trend from ViralScopes Opportunity Engine data
3. One creator tool/workflow tip

### Paid Advertising

`[ASSUMPTION]` Paid advertising is deliberately limited in Phase 1–2. CAC must be validated from organic channels before paid spend is scaled.

| Channel | Test budget | When | Targeting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twitter/X Promoted | £500/month | Month 6 test | Creator followers, marketing, SaaS |
| YouTube Pre-roll | £500/month | Month 9 test | Creator how-to video viewers |
| Google Ads | £1,000/month | Month 12 | High-intent branded + competitor keywords |
| LinkedIn Ads | £500/month | Month 9 test | Marketing managers, agency titles |

Paid channels are evaluated at 90 days. If CAC > 3× organic CAC for the same LTV cohort, the channel is paused.

### Referral & Affiliate Programme

**Referral (built-in to product, v1.5):**
- Existing customers get a unique referral link
- Referrer earns: 1 month free for each paying signup
- Referee earns: 20% off first month
- Tracking: first-click attribution via UTM + database lookup

**Affiliate programme (formal, v1.5):**
- Affiliates: creator educators, YouTube strategy newsletters, podcast hosts
- Commission: 20% of MRR for 12 months per referred paying customer
- Cookie window: 60 days
- Minimum payout: £50 (via bank transfer or PayPal)
- Tracked via Rewardful or Partnerstack `[ASSUMPTION]`

### Partnerships

**Creator educator partnerships:**
Target 10 creator educators with audiences > 50,000 in the YouTube/content creator space. Offer:
- Free Professional plan account
- Co-branded content (they use ViralScopes data in their videos/posts)
- Affiliate commission on referred customers

**Agency partnerships:**
Target 20 creator agencies in the UK and US. Offer:
- Agency pricing (volume discount for multi-seat Business plans)
- White-label case studies for their client presentations
- Priority access to new features

**Tool integrations (Phase 3):**
- Notion integration: push ViralScopes insights into content planning databases
- Airtable integration: sync trend and opportunity data
- Zapier/Make connector: connect ViralScopes to any workflow

## 4. Growth Metrics

### North Star Metric

**Weekly Active Organisations** — the number of distinct organisations that visit the dashboard and view at least one piece of AI-generated analysis in a given week. This metric captures both acquisition and engagement in a single number.

### Acquisition Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target (Month 12) |
|---|---|---|
| Organic sessions/month | GA4 organic traffic | 10,000 |
| Signups/month | New accounts created | 400 |
| Free plan activations | Signups who view at least 3 videos | 60% of signups |
| MQL (marketing qualified leads) | Signups who trigger 3+ engagement events | 100/month |

### Conversion Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Free → paid conversion | % of free users who upgrade within 30 days | 8% |
| Trial conversion (if applicable) | % of trials that convert to paid | 35% |
| Paywall hit rate | % of users who hit a paid feature limit | 25% of free users |
| CAC (blended) | Total sales + marketing spend ÷ new customers | < £150 |
| CAC payback period | Months to recover CAC | < 4 months |

### Revenue Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Target (Month 12) |
|---|---|---|
| MRR | Sum of all active subscription amounts | £6,150 |
| ARR | MRR × 12 | £73,800 |
| ARPU | MRR ÷ paying customers | £55 |
| Expansion MRR | MRR from plan upgrades | 15% of total MRR |

### Retention Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly churn | Customers lost ÷ customers at start of month | < 5% |
| Revenue churn | MRR lost ÷ MRR at start of month | < 4% |
| NRR | (MRR + expansion - churn) ÷ starting MRR | > 110% |
| NPS | Net Promoter Score | > 40 |

### Channel Metrics (Tracked Monthly)

| Channel | Primary metric | Secondary metric |
|---|---|---|
| SEO | Organic sessions | Keyword rankings |
| Email | Open rate | Click-to-signup rate |
| Affiliate | Referred signups | Affiliate MRR |
| Social | Follower growth | Profile clicks |
| Paid | CAC | ROAS |
| Product Hunt | Day-1 upvotes | Signups from launch |
